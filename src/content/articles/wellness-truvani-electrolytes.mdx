Hydration has officially become one of wellness' biggest conversations. With dozens of electrolyte powders now lining store shelves and Instagram feeds alike, the question has shifted — it's no longer whether you should be drinking one, it's which one is actually worth the money.

That's exactly the gap Truvani is stepping into. The brand has built its reputation on clean-label formulations and full ingredient transparency, positioning itself as the electrolyte option for people who read the back of the label before the front. In a category that's gotten crowded with sugar-loaded, dye-heavy formulas dressed up in wellness branding, that kind of scrutiny matters.

Whether Truvani's version becomes a genuine daily staple or just another pantry trial will come down to taste and how it actually performs mid-workout — but the brand's track record on formulation earns it the benefit of the doubt.

We'll report back once we've put it through a real week of testing.
