Nike stores are now carrying SKIMS essentials, the product of a collaboration that merges Nike's performance technology with SKIMS' body-inclusive, fit-obsessed design approach.

The pairing makes sense on paper: Nike brings decades of fabric and performance R&D, SKIMS brings a sizing and fit philosophy that's resonated with a customer who's felt underserved by traditional activewear cuts. Landing the line in physical Nike stores — rather than keeping it an online-only drop — puts it in front of a much broader, more casual shopper than either brand could reach alone.

For anyone who's found themselves choosing between Nike's technical performance and a genuinely well-fitting silhouette, this collaboration is worth a look. Early pieces are already moving fast in-store.
