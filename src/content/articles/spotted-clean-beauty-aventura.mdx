The clean beauty destination that's become a fixture for Miami Beach's skincare-obsessed is opening a second location in Aventura — and it's a smart bet on where the clean beauty conversation is headed next.

Clean beauty retail has shifted from a niche shelf category to a full-blown lifestyle destination, with curated retailers doing the work of separating genuinely effective, ingredient-conscious formulas from greenwashed noise. That curation is the whole value proposition, and it's exactly what's built this brand's following on the Beach.

Aventura makes sense as the next stop: dense retail traffic, a shopper who already treats beauty as a considered purchase, and — with the mall's ongoing wave of new openings — a neighborhood that's clearly positioning itself as South Florida's next beauty and wellness retail corridor.

We'll be first in line when the doors open.
