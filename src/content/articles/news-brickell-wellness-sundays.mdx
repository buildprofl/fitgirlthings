Brickell City Centre is launching a recurring Sunday wellness series, curated to turn the shopping center into a weekly workout destination rather than just a place to run errands.

The move fits a broader pattern: retail centers increasingly treating community programming as core to the experience rather than an occasional bonus event. A recurring Sunday series — rather than a one-off activation — signals real investment in building a habit, the kind of "see you next week" cadence that boutique studios have used for years to build loyalty.

For a neighborhood that's already dense with fitness studios and wellness retail, a free (or low-cost) recurring event at the center of it all is a smart way to pull foot traffic from across the format spectrum — reformer regulars, run club types, and casual Sunday movers alike.
