SET ACTIVE is landing in Miami this weekend with a limited-run "Club SET" pop-up — part retail activation, part community event, and a preview of how the activewear brand wants to show up in new markets before committing to permanent retail.

Pop-ups like this have become the go-to move for direct-to-consumer activewear brands testing a city before signing a lease: low commitment, high visibility, and a built-in excuse to throw a party. Expect the space to lean heavily into the brand's social-first identity — think curated moment, not quiet showroom.

If the turnout matches the brand's online following, don't be surprised if this becomes the opening chapter of a permanent Miami footprint.
