The Pilates Place Studios is celebrating 20 years in business — a genuine milestone in a fitness landscape where most studios are lucky to see five, let alone two decades.

Twenty years means this studio was doing serious mat and reformer work in Miami long before Pilates became the cultural juggernaut it is today. That kind of longevity earns a different kind of credibility than the wave of newer, better-funded studios currently expanding across the city — this is the brand a lot of Miami's current Pilates instructors likely trained under, or at least trained alongside, at some point in their own careers.

As the reformer studio boom keeps accelerating with new national and boutique brands landing every few months, it's worth pausing to note the ones that were doing the work before it was trendy. The Pilates Place has earned its place as one of the city's foundational studios.

Here's to the next twenty.
