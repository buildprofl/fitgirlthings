The pandemic broke a lot of things. It also permanently rewired how women socialize.

Bars stopped being the default. Restaurants got expensive and loud. Instead, a new social geometry formed around **shared micro-experiences**: 7 a.m. Pilates, matcha at the counter after, a recovery club appointment on Thursday, a run club on Saturday.

## The new third places

Not the office. Not the bar. The reformer studio, the sauna house, the walk-club coffee stop.

## Why it works

- **Low-friction.** No dress code, no menu anxiety.
- **Aligned energy.** Everyone showed up wanting the same thing.
- **Repeatable.** Weekly cadence builds actual friendships, not one-off conversations.

## What operators should know

The wellness girl is no longer paying only for the workout. She's paying for the **room**. Studios that understand this — the ones with intentional aesthetic, a real social element after class, a group of regulars she'd want to be friends with — win.

Studios that treat class like a transaction die slowly.
