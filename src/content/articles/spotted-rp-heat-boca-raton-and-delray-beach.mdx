Barely a few months after opening its first studio, RP Heat is already scaling. The heated mat Pilates concept — a spinoff of the Reforming Pilates brand — is bringing its signature format to Boca Raton and Delray Beach, extending its footprint north from its Fort Lauderdale debut.

The format pairs a warmed studio with a low-impact, high-output mat sequence, the kind of workout that's become a staple for anyone chasing a serious sweat without the joint stress of higher-impact training. It's the same instinct that's driven the broader reformer and mat Pilates boom across South Florida over the past two years.

Two locations in one expansion is an aggressive pace, and it says something about how fast this format found its audience. If the Fort Lauderdale opening is any indication, expect the Boca and Delray studios to fill their early class slots fast.
