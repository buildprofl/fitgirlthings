Sometimes wellness looks like a two-hour prep sesh with mise en place and a butcher block. Most days it looks like whatever you can blitz in three minutes before Pilates.

These smoothies are for the second kind of day.

## Rule one: protein first

Skip the fruit-only blends. Twenty grams of protein plus fiber is the difference between "held me over" and "hungry by 10 a.m."

## The three-ingredient formula

- **1 scoop protein** (whey or plant, unsweetened)
- **1 cup fluid** (oat milk, coconut water, or just water)
- **1 flavor anchor** (frozen banana, wild blueberries, or a spoonful of nut butter)

Blend, done. Optional add: chia, greens powder, or half an avocado for staying power.

## Three combos we keep coming back to

1. **The Pilates Recovery** — vanilla protein + coconut water + frozen mango + fresh mint
2. **The Cold-Plunge Chaser** — chocolate protein + oat milk + banana + almond butter + espresso shot
3. **The Boardroom Fix** — vanilla protein + almond milk + wild blueberries + spinach + chia
