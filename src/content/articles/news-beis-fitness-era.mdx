Béis — the travel brand co-founded by Shay Mitchell that's become a fixture at every airport gate — is expanding into activewear with a new lifestyle-focused collection.

It's a logical next step for a brand that's built its identity on function-first design and a distinctly Instagrammable neutral palette. Travel and fitness have always overlapped more than people give them credit for — the same person hauling a Béis suitcase through security is often the one squeezing in an airport-lounge workout or a hotel gym session before a big meeting — so a move into activewear reads less like a stretch and more like the brand finally addressing a gap in its own product story.

Whether Béis can compete with the activewear category's already-crowded field of direct-to-consumer challengers remains to be seen, but the brand's existing following gives it a real head start most new entrants don't have.
