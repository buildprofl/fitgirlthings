Faces of Fitness is bringing a two-day wellness festival to Coconut Grove this October, joining the growing wave of multi-day fitness experiences that have turned a single workout class into a full weekend event.

The format has proven itself elsewhere: stack workouts, panels, recovery experiences, and brand activations into one weekend, and you get something that reads more like a wellness-industry homecoming than a typical fitness class. For a city like Miami — with no shortage of studios, trainers, and brands but relatively few events that bring the whole scene together in one place — a festival like this fills an obvious gap.

Coconut Grove is a fitting host neighborhood, with the kind of outdoor space and existing wellness density that a festival of this scale needs to work.

Full lineup and ticket details are expected as the October dates approach — we'll have updates as they land.
