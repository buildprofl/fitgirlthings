Pause is opening in Brickell, bringing a dedicated recovery studio to a neighborhood that's mostly been defined by its reformer studios and boutique gyms — not the cold plunges, compression, and infrared that recovery culture actually runs on.

That's the gap Pause is stepping into. Recovery has gone from an afterthought tacked onto a gym membership to its own category, with dedicated studios built around the idea that how you recover matters as much as how you train. Brickell's dense, high-intensity work culture makes it an obvious testing ground: a population that trains hard and works longer hours than almost anywhere else in Miami is exactly who a recovery-first studio is built for.

Early chatter in recovery circles suggests this opening has been anticipated for a while — the kind of studio people have been asking "when is Brickell finally getting one of these" about.
