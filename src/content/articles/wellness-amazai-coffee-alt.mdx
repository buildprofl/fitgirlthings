Caffeine reduction has quietly become one of wellness' more persistent conversations — not full elimination, just a genuine interest in taking the edge off the afternoon crash without giving up the ritual that makes coffee feel non-negotiable in the first place.

AmaZai has positioned itself squarely in that gap. Rather than trying to replicate coffee's flavor exactly, it leans into its own identity as a lower-caffeine alternative that still delivers the ritual — something warm, something to hold, something that marks the start of the day — without the jitters or crash that come with a second or third cup.

The coffee-alternative category has had plenty of false starts over the years, mostly from products that nailed the health angle but missed on taste. Early buzz around AmaZai suggests it's closing that gap better than most.

Worth trying if your relationship with your third cup of coffee has started to feel complicated.
