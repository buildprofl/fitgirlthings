SWEAT440 is opening in Pinecrest, bringing its signature 40-minute, fully coached, station-based workout to one of Miami's more residential — and notoriously time-strapped — neighborhoods.

The format's whole pitch is built around the calendar problem: no 90-minute studio commitment, no wasted warm-up time figuring out equipment, just four 10-minute stations and a coach keeping the group moving. For a Pinecrest crowd juggling school pickups, commutes, and everything in between, a workout that respects the clock is the actual differentiator — not another amenity, not another smoothie bar, just time back.

It's also a sign of where boutique fitness expansion is heading generally: out of the dense urban core and into the neighborhoods where the target member actually lives, rather than assuming everyone will commute downtown for a workout.
