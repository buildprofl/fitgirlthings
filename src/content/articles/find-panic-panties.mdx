Some Fit Girl Finds are about discovering something new. This one is about finally admitting you need something you've probably needed for years: a backup pair, always in the bag, for the day that doesn't go as planned.

Panic Panties earn their name honestly — they're the specialty undergarment made for exactly the scenario every busy woman knows: workout runs long, plans change last minute, or the day just takes an unexpected turn, and suddenly you need to transition straight from the studio into the rest of your schedule with zero time to go home first.

It's a small addition to the gym bag, but it's the kind of small addition that solves a real, recurring problem rather than just adding clutter. That's the whole Fit Girl Find standard.

Keep one in every bag. You'll use it more than you'd think.
