The post-workout window is prime real estate. Skin is warm, circulation is up, pores are open, product absorbs better. Most people waste it wiping down with a gym towel and calling it done.

## The five-minute post-class routine

1. **Rinse, don't scrub.** Warm water, gentle cleanser. Full skincare can wait for actual home.
2. **Hydrating mist.** Not toner. Something with panthenol or glycerin, spritzed right after the rinse.
3. **Barrier serum.** One or two drops. Look for niacinamide + peptides.
4. **SPF.** Non-negotiable if there's daylight left.
5. **Lip balm.** Chapstick doesn't count. Something with ceramides.

## The Sunday reset

Once a week: 20-minute steam (sauna, or hot shower with the door closed), a proper mask (clay if congested, gel if depleted), and a scalp oil massage before bed. Ten dollars in ingredients, spa-level payoff.

## The things not worth doing

- Lymphatic drainage tools that aren't gua sha
- Overpriced "workout skincare" — regular skincare works
- Anything that promises "detox"
