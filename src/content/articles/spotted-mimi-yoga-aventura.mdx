Mimi Yoga is giving Miami its first look at the studio's upcoming Aventura location, with a full opening planned for April.

What sets Mimi Yoga apart in an increasingly crowded studio landscape is the founder-led feel — the kind of small-batch attention to class design and community that's harder to maintain once a brand scales into a multi-location operation. The Aventura studio is the brand's first real test of whether that intimacy survives expansion.

Early looks at the space suggest a design language that leans warm and intentional rather than the sterile, high-gloss aesthetic that's become the default in boutique fitness build-outs. If the practice matches the preview, Mimi Yoga could carve out a genuinely distinct lane in a neighborhood that's rapidly filling up with wellness concepts.

We'll have the full opening details as April approaches.
