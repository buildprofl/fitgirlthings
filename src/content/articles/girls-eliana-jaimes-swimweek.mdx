Confidence looks good on her.

Miami Swim Week is here, and this year it means something a little different for Eliana Jaimes — her first time stepping onto the runway. It's the kind of moment that reads as glamorous from the outside, but for Eliana, it's the visible end point of a much longer, quieter process of healing and self-trust built through consistent training and a willingness to say yes to things that genuinely scare her.

That's the throughline in how she talks about her fitness journey: not chasing a look, but chasing the feeling of showing up for herself, session after session, until the fear of a first runway walk became something she could actually stand inside of rather than run from.

There's something instructive in that for anyone watching from the audience — the runway moment isn't the story. The training, the discipline, and the decision to keep saying yes anyway is.

We'll be front row cheering her on.
