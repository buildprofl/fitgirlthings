Tickets are officially on sale for Faces of Fitness Miami — the two-day wellness festival hitting Coconut Grove October 24 and 25 — and this year's lineup is the strongest yet, with Dwyane Wade leading the local roster.

The festival format keeps proving itself: workouts, wellness experiences, live music, recovery activations, and brand partnerships packed into a single weekend, with enough marquee names attached to pull people who'd never normally sign up for a fitness event. A name like Wade's does real work here — it turns a niche industry gathering into something with genuine mainstream pull, the kind of ticket that ends up in group chats well outside the usual wellness crowd.

For a city with as many trainers, studios, and brands as Miami, a festival that finally brings the whole ecosystem into one weekend has been a long time coming. Early ticket movement suggests this one's going to sell out well before the October dates arrive.

Full schedule and additional lineup names expected in the coming weeks.
