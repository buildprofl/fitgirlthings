Fit Girl Problems #002: when was the last time you actually cleaned your Pilates or yoga mat? Not the quick studio spray between classes. Not the half-hearted wipe because the next session was already lined up. Actually cleaned it.

Most people don't — and after enough hot, sweaty reformer sessions or heated mat classes, it shows. A proper mat refresher designed specifically for the material (rather than a generic all-purpose cleaner that can break down the surface over time) makes a bigger difference than most people expect, both for hygiene and for how long the mat actually lasts.

It's a small, unglamorous fix, but it's the kind of gym-bag addition that pays off every single class once you start using it.

Consider this your reminder — and your excuse to finally deal with it.
