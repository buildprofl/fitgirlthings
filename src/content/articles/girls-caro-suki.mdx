Some trainers build a client list. Caro Suki built a following — and there's a real difference between the two.

Caro has become one of Miami's most recognizable fitness personalities, the kind of instructor whose energy is instantly identifiable the moment she walks into a room. But what's kept her at the center of the city's fitness culture isn't just charisma — it's a genuine gift for building community around her training, turning a room of strangers into a group that shows up for each other as much as for the workout.

That "it-girl" label gets thrown around loosely in fitness circles, but in Caro's case it tracks: she's shaped how a whole generation of Miami's active women think about what a workout class should feel like — social, high-energy, and unmistakably fun, without sacrificing the intensity that keeps people coming back.

We sat down with Caro to talk about how she built her following, and what's next.
