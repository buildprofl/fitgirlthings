Lululemon is opening a large-format store at Brickell City Centre this summer — a significant investment in a neighborhood that's already become one of Miami's most competitive retail corridors for activewear and wellness brands.

A flagship-scale store typically means more than just extra square footage. Expect expanded categories, community programming (Lululemon has leaned hard into in-store classes and events at its bigger locations elsewhere), and a retail experience built to anchor the brand's Miami presence rather than just serve foot traffic.

Brickell City Centre has been quietly assembling one of the city's strongest wellness-adjacent retail rosters, and a Lululemon of this size is as much a statement about the center's positioning as it is about the brand itself.

Opening date to be confirmed — we'll be there when the doors open.
