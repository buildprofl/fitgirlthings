There are three tribes of fit girls right now. Pick your team.

## Pilates Princess

Signs you're one of us: monthly reformer membership, matcha every morning, at least two grip-sock colors. You believe in long, lean, and effortful-looking-effortless.

## Studio Loyalist

Signs: you're on a first-name basis with three instructors, you book the same 6 a.m. slot every Tuesday, and you've defended your studio like it was your alma mater.

## Outdoor Sweat Girl

Signs: run club Saturday, trail on Sunday, real weather doesn't stop you, and your Strava is a small identity statement.

None is better. All three are welcome here.

*Which one are you? Forward this to the friend who's the opposite tribe.*
