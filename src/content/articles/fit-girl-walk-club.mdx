The best events aren't the loudest. They're the ones that are easy to say yes to.

**Fit Girl Walk Club** started as a Saturday morning idea: meet at a coffee shop, walk for 40 minutes, come back and hang. No pressure, no pace requirement, no dress code beyond "you want to be outside."

Six months later it's the thing regulars build their weekend around.

## What made it work

- **A specific meet spot.** No "somewhere in the park." One café, one bench.
- **A hard end time.** Nobody wonders how long they're committing.
- **Coffee ritual.** The walk is the excuse. The lingering is the point.
- **Consistent cadence.** Same day, same time, every week.
