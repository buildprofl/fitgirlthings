Some instructors teach movement. Liany Blum creates transformation.

Born and raised in Miami, Liany has spent seven years building a career rooted in strength, intention, and genuine connection with her clients — the kind of instructor whose classes people rearrange their schedules around, not because of the workout format, but because of how she runs the room.

What comes through most in talking to her is how deliberately she treats "showing up" as the actual work. Not the perfect form, not the personal record, just the decision to be present and consistent, week after week — the unglamorous discipline that actually produces results over time.

Seven years in an industry that churns through instructors quickly is its own kind of proof. Liany has built something that lasts because she's never treated the job as just cueing reps — she's treated it as building people up, one session at a time.

Catch her teaching around Miami, where her classes remain some of the most consistently booked in the city.
