Hydration powders have been having a moment for a while now, but most stop at the "electrolytes for your workout" pitch and leave it there. HackU is doing something different — merging the hydration functionality with beauty-forward benefits, positioning the daily powder as a skincare ritual as much as a recovery tool.

It's a smart repositioning. Hydration genuinely does show up in skin — the connection between how much water (and how efficiently your body retains it) and how your skin looks is well understood, even if most electrolyte brands don't bother making the connection explicit. HackU leans into it, framing each scoop as part of a beauty routine rather than just a post-workout add-on.

For anyone already electrolyte-pilled who wants their daily ritual to feel a little more intentional, this is worth the switch.

We'll be tracking how it holds up over a full month of daily use.
