Miami Swim Week is usually built around one question: what are we wearing in the water? This year, Carve Designs showed up with a different one — what are we wearing for everything else?

The brand's Swim Week presence leaned into versatility over spectacle, positioning pieces that move seamlessly from the pool to a beach club lunch to a golden-hour walk without a wardrobe change in between. It's a quieter kind of statement than the runway-first drama Swim Week usually rewards, but it taps into something real: most people don't actually live their whole day in a swimsuit built only for swimming.

That "from Pilates to poolside" framing says a lot about where the brand is positioning itself — not purely swimwear, but an entire active-to-leisure category that's been growing steadily as the lines between workout clothes, resort wear, and everyday basics keep dissolving.

Expect to see more of this cross-category thinking from swimwear brands going forward.
