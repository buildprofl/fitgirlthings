BODYROK is bringing its reformer-based, high-intensity low-impact format to Coconut Grove — its first South Florida location, and a clear bet that Miami's Pilates appetite still has room for another national player.

The format leans into a familiar formula for anyone who's followed the reformer boom: fast-paced choreography, spring-loaded resistance, and a full-body burn that trades the joint stress of high-impact cardio for controlled, precise movement. What differentiates BODYROK nationally has been its consistency — a studio in Coconut Grove should feel recognizably the same as one anywhere else in the country, which matters to members who travel or split time between cities.

Coconut Grove itself has quietly become one of Miami's more competitive boutique fitness corridors, and a well-capitalized national brand setting up shop there is as much a signal about the neighborhood as it is about the format.

Booking details to come as the studio finalizes its opening.
