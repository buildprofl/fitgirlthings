ResortPass — the platform known for giving locals access to luxury hotel amenities without booking an overnight stay — has launched Off Hours, a new event series in partnership with Hilton. The idea: turn some of Florida's most beautiful hotel pools into full wellness, fitness, and lifestyle experiences.

It's a smart evolution for a platform that's already built its business on unlocking hotel amenities for day use. Rather than just selling pool access, Off Hours programs the experience — movement classes, wellness activations, and the kind of resort-caliber setting that's usually reserved for overnight guests.

For Hilton, it's a way to activate underused daytime pool hours and introduce the brand to a local audience that might never otherwise book a room. For everyone else, it's one more sign that the line between "hotel amenity" and "wellness event" keeps getting blurrier — in a good way.

We'll be watching to see which properties join the series next.
