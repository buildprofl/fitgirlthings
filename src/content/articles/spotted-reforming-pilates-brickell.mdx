Reforming Pilates — the brand behind the heated mat format now expanding across South Florida under the RP Heat name — is bringing its original reformer studio experience to Brickell.

It's a natural next step. Brickell's daytime population has exploded with the neighborhood's build-out, and the demand for a studio that can serve a lunch-break or after-work crowd without sacrificing quality has been obvious for a while. Reforming Pilates has built its reputation on precision instruction and a studio environment that feels more boutique hotel than gym, and Brickell is exactly the kind of dense, design-conscious neighborhood that rewards that positioning.

Between this and the RP Heat rollout in Boca Raton and Delray Beach, Reforming Pilates is having a real moment — moving from a single well-loved studio to a genuine multi-location brand in the span of a year.
