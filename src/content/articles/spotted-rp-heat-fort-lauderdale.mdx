Reforming Pilates is launching its heated mat concept under a new name — RP Heat — with its first studio opening in Fort Lauderdale this April.

The idea is simple on paper: take a proven mat Pilates sequence and run it in a warmed room, borrowing the muscle-loosening, sweat-inducing benefits of a heated studio without moving into higher-impact territory. It's a format that splits the difference between the reformer studios that dominate right now and the hot yoga rooms that came before them.

Given how quickly the parent Reforming Pilates brand has built a following on precision and studio design, expectations for this Fort Lauderdale debut are high. If it performs the way early buzz suggests, don't be surprised if RP Heat becomes the brand's primary growth vehicle across the rest of South Florida.

We'll be tracking the opening closely.
